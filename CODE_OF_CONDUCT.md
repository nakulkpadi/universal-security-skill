# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a professional, welcoming place for defensive security collaboration. Be respectful, focus criticism on ideas and evidence, avoid harassment or discrimination, and never use the project to encourage unauthorized access or harm.

## Expected behavior

- Share reproducible, defensive evidence.
- Respect privacy and never post real secrets or personal data.
- Disclose security issues privately according to `SECURITY.md`.
- Give credit for meaningful contributions and upstream work.
- Assume good faith while remaining precise about technical claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, posting credentials, encouraging unauthorized exploitation, or deliberately disrupting the project/community are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users. For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
